## Energy Consumption Analysis: May 2024 - May 2025

### Key Findings:

1. **Total Annual Consumption:** 5,790 kWh costing $694.80
2. **Average Monthly Consumption:** 482.5 kWh
3. **Highest Usage Month:** August 2024 (580 kWh)
4. **Lowest Usage Month:** May 2025 (410 kWh)

### Major Energy Consumers:
- Air Conditioner: 1,690.7 kWh (29.2% of total)
- Refrigerator: 1,294 kWh (22.3% of total)
- Washing Machine: 958.9 kWh (16.6% of total)

### Seasonal Patterns:
- Summer months (June-August) show highest consumption due to air conditioning
- Winter months show increased heating and lighting usage
- Spring months generally have the lowest overall consumption

### Potential Annual Savings:
- Total potential savings: 1,199.5 kWh ($143.94)
- Biggest opportunity: LED lighting upgrades could save $73.38 annually
- Simple behavioral changes like thermostat adjustments could save $20.29 annually
